# Offline sync and automatic conflict resolution are here

You're on a plane or in a subway tunnel, or the Wi-Fi is bad, and an idea strikes. You write it down and then wonder whether it will ever reach your other devices. As of today you can stop wondering.

## What's new

Our note-taking app now syncs offline edits and resolves conflicts automatically.

- **Offline editing.** Write, edit and organize notes with no connection. Changes save on your device and sync when you're back online.
- **Conflict resolution.** If you edited the same note on your phone and your tablet, our new merge engine combines the changes. You don't lose work, and you don't have to pick which version wins.

Your notes match on every device, however and whenever you edit them.

## Availability

Both features ship today on iOS and Android. Update the app to get them. Desktop follows next month.

## Try it

Switch on airplane mode, make a few edits, then reconnect and watch them merge in.

We'd like to hear how it works for you. Thanks for using the app, and happy note-taking.
