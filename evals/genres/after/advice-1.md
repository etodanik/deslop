# Perspective on your situation

You want to help Leah, but your husband has strong feelings about financial independence and pride.

## Both sides

Chris seems to hold firm principles about money, possibly tied to how he sees himself as a provider. His stance may look rigid, but it likely comes from mixed feelings about responsibility, about parenting, and maybe about his previous relationship.

You want to use the resources you have to keep a child you love out of unnecessary debt, and that shows how much you care about Leah.

## Possible approaches

1. **Talk with Chris about his reasoning.** Instead of calling him "ridiculous and immature," try to understand what worries him. Is he trying to teach independence? Does he feel inadequate? Is he afraid of setting a precedent?

2. **Look for a compromise**, such as:
   - Offering to help with part of the costs, not all of them
   - Setting up a formal loan agreement if that would make him more comfortable
   - Asking whether your parents might offer a merit scholarship that acknowledges Leah
