# Making friends as an adult with little time or money

It's hard to build friendships as an adult, especially when people keep leaving and you have almost no spare time or money. A few ideas that might help:

## Low-cost, low-time options

1. **Your neighborhood.** Take short walks and start greeting neighbors regularly. Brief, repeated hellos can turn into friendships.

2. **Free community events.** Libraries, parks and community centers run free events you could attend for just an hour.

3. **Local online groups.** Join local Facebook groups or Nextdoor, where you might meet people nearby who share your interests.

4. **Micro-volunteering.** Some volunteer roles need as little as an hour a month and put you among people with similar interests.

## Work

Even if your coworkers aren't natural friend matches, you could:
- Suggest a quick coffee break with someone you find interesting
- Join workplace initiatives that match your values
