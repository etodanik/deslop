# Your Notes, Everywhere: Offline Sync and Automatic Conflict Resolution Are Here

We have all been there: you're on a plane, in a subway tunnel, or somewhere with spotty Wi-Fi, and inspiration strikes. You jot down a note, only to wonder later whether it will make it to your other devices. Today, we're thrilled to announce that worry is a thing of the past.

## What's New

Starting today, our note-taking app now **syncs offline edits** seamlessly and **resolves conflicts automatically**.

- **Offline editing, fully supported.** Keep writing, editing, and organizing your notes even without a connection. Your changes are saved locally and sync the moment you're back online.
- **Smart conflict resolution.** Edited the same note on your phone and tablet? No problem. Our new merge engine intelligently combines changes so you never lose your work, and you never have to manually pick a winner.
- **Peace of mind.** Your notes stay consistent across all your devices, no matter how or when you edit them.

## Availability

Offline sync and automatic conflict resolution are rolling out today on **iOS and Android**. Simply update the app to the latest version to get started. Desktop support is coming next month, so stay tuned.

## Try It Out

Update your app today and put it to the test. Switch on airplane mode, make a few edits, and watch everything come together when you reconnect.
