# Making Friends as an Adult with Limited Time and Resources

I understand how challenging it can be to build lasting friendships as an adult, especially with your history of people leaving and your current time and financial constraints. Here are some practical suggestions that might help:

## Low-Cost/Low-Time Commitment Options

1. **Neighborhood connections**: Take short walks around your neighborhood and start greeting neighbors regularly. Brief, consistent interactions can gradually build into friendships.

2. **Free community events**: Look for free events at local libraries, parks, or community centers that you could attend for even just an hour.

3. **Online communities with offline potential**: Join local Facebook groups or Nextdoor where you might connect with people in your area who share interests.

4. **Micro-volunteering**: Some volunteer opportunities require as little as an hour a month and can connect you with like-minded people.

## Workplace Connections

Even if your coworkers aren't natural friend matches, consider:
- Suggesting a quick coffee break with someone you find interesting
- Joining workplace initiatives that align with your values
