The tavern was loud. Someone near the fire was losing at dice and taking it badly, and the floor stuck to her boots. Mira kept her elbows in and her hood up until she reached the back.

Tomas had the corner table, the one with the short leg. He'd wedged a folded playing card under it. He didn't stand.

"You came," he said.

"You said it was about my brother."

He pushed a mug toward her. She didn't take it. On the walk over she'd decided she would listen, say nothing, and leave before the bell. Now she sat down anyway, and hated that he'd known she would.
